Help and FAQs

How do I create an account?

Click "Log in / Sign up", enter your name, email and a password, then press Sign up.

How do I join a course?

Log in, open your dashboard, find the course under "Available courses" and press Enrol.

How do exams work?

Open an enrolled course, choose an exam and press Start. A timer begins and cannot be paused. You can take each exam once, and your score appears as soon as you submit.

How do I submit an assignment?

Open the assignment in your course, choose your file (PDF, Word or a photo, up to 10 MB) and press Submit. You can replace it until it is marked.

How do I get my certificate?

Take every exam, submit every assignment (all marked), and reach the course pass mark. A "Get my certificate" button then appears on the course page.

I am a teacher. How do I get teacher access?

Sign up as normal, then ask the administrator to upgrade your account.
